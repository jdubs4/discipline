# Daily Discipline

A personal routine, journal and breathing app for iPhone that runs from your home screen.

## Put it online (GitHub Pages, free)
1. On github.com, create a new **public** repository, e.g. `discipline`.
2. Tap **Add file → Upload files** and upload everything in this folder.
3. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under "Branch", choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then tap **Save**.
4. After a minute or two, your app is live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/discipline/`.

## Install on iPhone
1. Open that link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
3. Use the app from the icon from then on. The home-screen app keeps its own data, separate from Safari.

Your data stays on your phone. Use **Settings → Export backup** now and then.
